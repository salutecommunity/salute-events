THE SALUTE SERIES
What is On the Table?
On the Table is SALUTE’s intimate dinner series—where the menu is the agenda.
Prompt-led courses guide conversation around shared interests and the different perspectives represented in the room.
Each evening closes with Give + Get, inviting guests to share what they can offer and what they are seeking—creating connections that continue beyond the table.
SALUTE is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and private professional network for South Asian women and allies.
