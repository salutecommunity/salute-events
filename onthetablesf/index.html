03 / The format

How the evening unfolds

The menu
is the agenda.

A dinner designed as a progression—from opening the conversation to creating connections that continue beyond the evening.

A set of conversation cards helps the table move beyond titles, familiar background questions, and the usual networking talk.

With each themed course, new prompts guide the conversation in a different direction and create space for genuine exchange.

The evening closes with Give + Get, where each guest shares something she can offer and something she would value as a starting point for connection beyond dinner.

06 / About SALUTE

South Asian
leadership
starts with us.

South Asian Leaders Unite to Empower (SALUTE) is a private professional network dedicated to creating impactful connections between South Asian women.

Our mission is to expand access, visibility, and connection for South Asian women and allies. Our members are executives, trailblazers, and pioneers who are shaping the present and influencing the future.

SALUTE is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.
