06 / About SALUTE

South Asian
leadership
starts with us.

South Asian Leaders Unite to Empower (SALUTE) is a private professional network dedicated to creating impactful connections between South Asian women.

Our mission is to expand access, visibility, and connection for South Asian women and allies. Our members are executives, trailblazers, and pioneers who are shaping the present and influencing the future.

SALUTE is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.
